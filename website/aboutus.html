Who We Are

Beautiful, relaxing, and
sustainable outdoor spaces

Bulakena Garden is a trusted garden shop and landscape services provider rooted in Talisay. Founded with a genuine love for plants and people, we've grown from a small family nursery into a full-service garden destination.

Through our web-based platform, customers can browse our curated plant collections, request professional services, and stay connected with our team all from the comfort of home.

Our Background

Every great garden starts with a seed of passion. Here's how ours grew into something the whole community calls its own.

From a Family Backyard
to Bulakena Garden

A Backyard Dream

It started in a modest Talisay home - potted plants lining every corner, cuttings shared with neighbors, and a growing obsession with making green spaces beautiful.

01
2012
02
2015

The First Nursery Opens

Word spread fast. Neighbors became customers, and Bulakena Garden opened its first proper nursery selling carefully propagated plants and offering practical gardening guidance.

Landscaping Services Launch

Clients kept asking "Can you also do our whole garden?" We said yes. Our landscaping team formed, taking on residential and commercial projects across Talisay and beyond.

03
2018
04
2022

Going Digital

We launched our facebook platform letting customers browse our selling plants and reach us online, making Bulakena Garden accessible to everyone.

Growing Into the Future

Today, Bulakena Garden continues to flourish expanding our collections, refining our services, and staying true to our roots: a family business that genuinely loves what it does.
